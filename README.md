## ✨ Features

- 💬 **Real-time chat**: one-to-one and group chats with messages that arrive instantly
- 🔔 **Notifications**: new-message alerts even when the app is closed
- 📊 **Polls**: ask a question, let everyone vote, and watch the results update live
- 🔥 **View-once photos and videos**: they open once, screenshots are blocked, then they're gone
- 🖼️ **Photos, videos, voice notes and files**
- 😄 **Reactions, replies, edits, forwarding and "delete for everyone"**
- ✅ **Read receipts, typing indicator and last seen**, with privacy controls
- 🎨 **Personalise everything**: themes, accent colours, bubble styles, wallpapers, text size and app icons
- 🗂️ **Chat folders and search**
- 🔐 **Sign in with Google** or with email
- ⬆️ **In-app updates**: Ember tells you when a new version is ready

## 📥 Install

1. Open the **[latest release](https://github.com/AqoussGaming/Ember-Messenger/releases/latest)** on your Android phone.
2. Under **Assets**, tap the `Ember-…apk` file to download it.
3. Open the download. If Android asks, allow your browser to **install unknown apps**.
4. Tap **Install**, then open Ember and create your account.

> Needs **Android 8.0 or newer**.
> After this first install, Ember updates itself: open **Settings → App updates**, or tap the prompt when a new version is out.

## 🛡️ Privacy

- Messages are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- You decide who sees your last seen and read receipts, and who can message you.
- View-once media can only be opened once and can't be screenshotted.
- There's no advertising and no analytics tracking.
